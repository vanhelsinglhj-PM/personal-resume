01 / CONTEXT

项目背景Background

轨道编辑页资源缩略图加载慢（贴纸、特效等颗粒缩略图加载速度可达8秒以上），且资源包体体积过大导致下载速度慢，严重影响用户编辑体验及操作效率。Thumbnails for timeline resources such as stickers and effects could take more than eight seconds to load. Oversized asset packages also slowed downloads, severely disrupting editing and efficiency.

02 / CHALLENGE

问题与挑战Challenges

1）轨道编辑页资源颗粒数量庞大——共143万个，效果验收工作量极大，常规人工验收无法满足效率需求；1) The timeline contained 1.43 million resource items, making manual quality validation impractical.

2）资源压缩无历史案例参考，且压缩后易出现效果失真风险，影响用户体验。2) There was no prior resource-compression precedent, and compression risked visual degradation and a worse user experience.

03 / APPROACH

优化思路Approach
与解决方案and solutions

01

优化验收流程：静态图采用人工抽检，动态图（webp、gif 格式）借助 CV 模型开发自动化脚本进行全量验收，将 4.6 万个动态图的验收工作量从 48 人日（0.5min / 个）压缩至 0.5 天，大幅提升效率；Improved validation: manually sample static images and use CV-based scripts to validate all animated WebP and GIF assets. This reduced verification of 46,000 animations from 48 person-days (0.5 minutes each) to 0.5 days.

02

联动应用层、中间层、音视频编辑底层开发团队，梳理包体结构，明确包体中未被应用的文件类型，为压缩优化提供依据；Worked with application, middleware and audio-video editing infrastructure teams to identify unused file types in asset packages and inform compression.

03

采用稳健推进策略，先抽取 2022 年 8 月后生产的核心类型颗粒 2% 进行试跑，通过自动化测试保障功能正常、人工抽检确认效果达标后，再对使用量前 90% 的颗粒进行全面压缩。Rolled out cautiously: first trial compression on 2% of core asset types produced after August 2022, validate function with automated tests and quality with manual sampling, then compress items accounting for the top 90% of usage.

04 / OUTCOME

项目成果Outcomes

节省70%云端存储空间，安卓平均加载速度提升18.51%，iOS平均加载速度提升31.48%，用户在轨道编辑页点击包体后预计双端提升下载速度30%。Saved 70% of cloud storage; average loading speed improved 18.51% on Android and 31.48% on iOS. Download speed after selecting a timeline asset package was estimated to improve by 30% on both platforms.
