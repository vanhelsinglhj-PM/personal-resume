01 / CONTEXT

项目背景Background

环球音乐在桌面端上线后因为单价高、不支持先试用再订阅所以用户在拖拽到时间线轨道前就会被付费弹窗拦截掉，试听下载率0.93%和曝光购买转化率0.48%，远低于其他类型的资源。Universal Music launched on desktop at a high price without a try-before-subscribe option. A paywall blocked users before they could drag music onto the timeline. Listening downloads were 0.93% and exposure-to-purchase conversion was 0.48%, well below other resources.

02 / CHALLENGE

问题与挑战Challenges

1）环球音乐不支持先试用再订阅且单价较高（非商用10.99/月、商用29.99/月），用户决策成本高；1) No trial and high prices ($10.99/month for non-commercial use; $29.99/month for commercial use) raised the decision cost.

2）试听前先订阅拦截掉绝大部分用户，导致大量潜在用户流失，无法完成转化闭环。2) Requiring a subscription before listening blocked most users, losing potential customers before they could convert.

03 / APPROACH

优化思路Approach
与解决方案and solutions

01

拉通商务同事与环球音乐谈妥7天0元试用和最低5折折扣的条款，解决环球音乐客单价过高的问题；Worked with the business team and Universal Music to agree on a seven-day free trial and discounts of up to 50%, addressing the high price.

02

将环球音乐订阅拦截后置到导出环节，允许用户先下载试听，完成编辑付出沉没成本后再进行付费拦截。Moved the subscription paywall to export, allowing users to download, listen and edit before encountering the paid subscription prompt.

04 / OUTCOME

项目成果Outcomes

↑7.6倍↑7.6×曝光购买转化率Exposure-to-purchase rate
↑54倍↑54×试听下载率Listening download rate
$50,000单月收入Monthly revenue

曝光购买转化率提升7.6倍（0.48%→4.13%）、试听下载率提升54倍（0.93%→51.74%），实现单月收入5万美金的项目目标，实现商业价值突破。Exposure-to-purchase conversion increased 7.6 times (0.48%→4.13%), listening downloads increased 54 times (0.93%→51.74%), and the project achieved its $50,000 monthly revenue target.
