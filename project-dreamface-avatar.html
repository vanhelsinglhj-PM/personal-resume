01 / CONTEXT

项目背景Background

DreamFace 原有 Avatar 功能存在明显的生成效果问题：免费玩法仅能驱动嘴部，缺乏身体动作；付费玩法虽能生成全身动作，但面部容易出现僵硬、扭曲和变形，影响作品质量与用户体验。DreamFace’s existing Avatar features had clear generation quality issues. The free option animated only the mouth and lacked body movement; the paid option generated full-body motion, but faces could become stiff, distorted or deformed, reducing video quality and user experience.

02 / CHALLENGE

问题与挑战Challenges

1）模型能力限制：免费玩法采用 GAN 模型进行唇形同步，无法驱动身体动作；付费玩法接入 OmniHuman 和 Wan 模型，但面部渲染容易出现变形、身份漂移等问题。1) Model limitations: The free experience used a GAN model for lip synchronization and could not drive body motion. The paid experience used OmniHuman and Wan, but face rendering could suffer from distortion and identity drift.

2）模型成本限制：受成本控制要求影响，方案只能采用开源或自研模型，不引入额外的第三方模型调用成本。2) Model costs: Cost constraints required open-source or in-house models, without additional third-party model fees.

3）用户体验问题：用户输入的音频文件及历史生成设置无法有效保留，影响重复创作体验。3) User experience: Uploaded audio and previous generation settings could not be retained effectively, making repeat creation cumbersome.

03 / APPROACH

优化思路Approach
与解决方案and solutions

01

混合渲染方案：采用 Wan 2.1 + GAN 的组合方式。用户上传单张图片后，先通过 Wan 2.1 模型结合内置提示词生成 5 秒底板视频，再利用 GAN 模型根据脚本音频生成唇形同步视频，最后将唇形同步结果与底板视频融合，输出嘴部与肢体动作协调的数字人视频。Hybrid rendering: Combine Wan 2.1 and GAN. From one uploaded image, Wan 2.1 and built-in prompts generate a five-second base video. A GAN then generates lip-synchronized video from the script audio; the results are merged to coordinate mouth and body motion.

02

使用体验优化：将用户上传的音频文件缓存在浏览器端，并在服务端保存历史生成设置，减少重复上传和配置操作，提升连续创作体验。Creation experience: Cache uploaded audio in the browser and save previous generation settings on the server, reducing repeated uploads and setup.
