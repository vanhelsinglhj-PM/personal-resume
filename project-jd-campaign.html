01 / CONTEXT

项目背景Background

京东大促营销会场站外流量占比高达 8 成，但站外用户转化意愿天然低于站内；运营人员因缺乏高效适配方案，常直接复用站内冗长会场页面，进一步加剧站外用户流失，制约大促整体转化目标达成。External traffic accounted for 80% of JD shopping festival visits, but those visitors had lower purchase intent. Without an efficient adaptation tool, operators often reused long on-site campaign pages, worsening drop-off and limiting conversion.

02 / CHALLENGE

问题与挑战Challenges

1）站内页面冗长，若直接复用站外用户极易流失；1) Long internal campaign pages risked losing external visitors when reused without adaptation.

2）沉淀为通用策略要进行AB实验，但有100多个一二级品类，若一个个进行AB实验效率极低。2) Validating reusable strategies with separate A/B tests across more than 100 categories and subcategories would be extremely inefficient.

03 / APPROACH

优化思路Approach
与解决方案and solutions

01

通过竞品分析和现状调研，沉淀出3条固化的策略至后台（矮头图+钩子商品优先展示+短动线），方便运营快速配置；Used competitor analysis and research to establish three reusable backend strategies: shorter hero images, priority placement of hook products, and shorter user journeys.

02

引入多变量正交实验大幅减少AB实验次数，并通过拉通家电和3C消费电子事业部进行试点，获得正向数据反馈后树立标杆向其他事业部进行推广。Introduced multivariable orthogonal experiments to reduce the number of A/B tests. Piloted with home appliances and 3C consumer electronics, then expanded to other business units after positive results.

04 / OUTCOME

项目成果Outcomes

590.7万5.907MGMV 增量Incremental GMV
↑19.37%UV 点击率Unique-visitor click-through rate
↑29.25%UV 转化率Unique-visitor conversion rate

在2021年双11的三个一级事业部会场进行应用，带来590.7万的GMV增量，UV点击率和UV转化率分别提升19.37%、29.25%，有效解决站外流量转化低效痛点，助力大促整体目标达成。Applied at three primary business-unit venues during the 2021 Singles’ Day campaign, generating RMB 5.907 million in incremental GMV and increasing unique-visitor click-through and conversion rates by 19.37% and 29.25%, respectively.
