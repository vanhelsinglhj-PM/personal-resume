01 / CONTEXT

项目背景Background

移动端原搜索推荐采用单一 ES 精准匹配模式，缺乏模糊匹配能力，导致需求明确用户体验不佳；同时存在本地化推荐不足、模板时效性失衡等问题，使得搜索渗透率常年仅 1%，搜索用户次日留存与大盘持平，未形成差异化优势。Mobile search used a single exact-match ES approach without fuzzy matching, frustrating users with clear needs. Weak localization and imbalanced template freshness kept search penetration around 1%, while next-day retention among search users matched the overall baseline.

02 / CHALLENGE

问题与挑战Challenges

1）搜索推荐缺少模糊匹配能力，召回率及准确率偏低；1) Search recommendations lacked fuzzy matching, resulting in low recall and relevance.

2）模板本地化适配不足，日韩地区会出现大量欧美人面孔；2) Localized templates were insufficient; users in Japan and Korea frequently saw Western faces.

3）模板曝光机制不合理——新上的模板由于缺少使用数据而无法优先曝光，过时的模板由于使用数据好而被优先曝光。3) The exposure mechanism favored older templates with historical usage data, while new templates lacked sufficient data to gain visibility.

03 / APPROACH

优化思路Approach
与解决方案and solutions

01

接入qwen3识别query语义，结合deepFM模型做推荐，提升模板召回率和准确率；Use Qwen3 to interpret query semantics and DeepFM for recommendations, improving template recall and relevance.

02

重构推荐分发机制，由全局资源池筛选改为语言标签优先分发——运营在后台为资源打上“可投放语言”标签，算法优先推荐对应资源池内模板；Shift from filtering a global pool to language-prioritized delivery: operations staff tag resources with eligible languages, and the algorithm prioritizes the corresponding pools.

03

根据常态化运营节奏，梳理模板时效性提权、降权规则，后台入库增加字段进行识别。Define freshness-based promotion and demotion rules in line with ongoing operations, adding fields to identify resources in the backend.
